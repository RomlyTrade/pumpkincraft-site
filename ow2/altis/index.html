SHOPS

Hold what you're selling, look at a chest or barrel and run /shop create <price>. Buyers right-click: their silver goes in, one item comes out. Restock and collect whenever — only you can see what it's earned.

BOSS ARENA

at the altar.

FIGHT

Thirty seconds' warning, announced to the whole server. Everyone can come. PvP stays on.

REWARD

The killing blow spins the wheel — not the summoner, not the group.

RAID SITES

Bastions and fortresses in the Nether, and the Ocean Monument, at fixed spots and rebuilt from scratch every Sunday 06:00 GMT. One set of chests — first there wins.

DROWNED

This is a PLAYER ENFORCED SERVER. There is only one admin rule: no cheating. Every other rule is up to the players, and /drown gives you the power to enforce them.

DROWN SOMEONE

/drown <player> costs 640 silver. They lose the ability to breathe air.

BREAK THE CURSE

The drowned have to grind 1024 dried kelp blocks to /buyout and breathe again.

RULES

NO MODDING

No add-ons that give you an unfair advantage.

NO EXPLOITING

No abusing bugs, pearling through walls and so on.
