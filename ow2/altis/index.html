CLAIMS & CURRENCY

CLAIMS

Our unique claims system works like so:

/claim will protect the cubic chunk you are stood in. That's 16×16×16.

After your first cubic chunk, you must place your barrel, which is where you pay your rent into.

If you do not pay your rent by Sunday 18:00 GMT, your claim will be dissolved, and no longer protected from other players.

SEEDS

Shmeckle Shmackers have no other source — no crafting, no shearing, no traders. /seed is the only way, and every seed you buy makes your next one dearer.

The ladder: 16, 20, 24, 28, 36, 40, 48, 60, 68, 84 SBT — tracked per player. Breaking a stem gives exactly one seed back, so a farm sustains itself but never multiplies for free.

SHOPS

Hold what you're selling, look at a chest or barrel and run /shop create <price>. Buyers right-click: their SBT goes in, one item comes out. Restock and collect whenever — only you can see what it's earned.

BOSS ARENA

PAY

Ender Dragon 512 · Warden 448 · Wither 384 SBT at the altar.

FIGHT

Thirty seconds' warning, announced to the whole server. Everyone can come. PvP stays on.

REWARD

The killing blow spins the wheel — not the summoner, not the group.

RAID SITES

Bastions and fortresses at fixed spots in the Nether, rebuilt from scratch every Sunday 06:00 GMT. One set of chests — first there wins. Bring gold.

DROWNING

TAKE SOMEONE'S AIR

/drown <player> costs 640 SBT. They surface somewhere deep and cold with a mangrove propagule, two sea lanterns and a furnace.

BUY YOUR LUNGS BACK

/buyout with 1024 dried kelp blocks in your inventory and you're returned to your bed.

Drowned players can swim anywhere wet but can't set foot on land. They lose global chat and talk in [Drowned], heard by anyone standing in water. Their overworld bases freeze for 7 days. Portals are closed to them, and dying puts them back in the water.
