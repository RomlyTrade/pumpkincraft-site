HOW A PLOT WORKS

THE SHAPE

A plot is three chunks in a row (48 × 16) and three cubes tall: underground, ground and sky. That's nine 16×16×16 cubes.

LOCKED CUBES

You start with one cube — the ground cube of the first chunk, your farm. The rest stay locked and protected from everyone, you included, until you upgrade.

CLAIMING

Stand inside a free plot and type /plot. Claiming costs 64 SBT from your inventory. One plot per player.

MEMBERS

Add friends from /plot. Members can build and farm in every unlocked cube and use the same menu.

Ice Shmeckle Shmackers (melon seeds) only grow inside plot farm cubes, so Stratis is the only place Frozen tokens come from.

RENT

DEPOSIT

The rent button in /plot deposits every SBT in your inventory into the plot's balance.

DAILY

Rent comes out at 00:00 GMT. If the balance can't cover it, the rest becomes debt. Deposits clear debt first.

SUNDAY

Still in debt at Sunday 18:00 GMT? The plot is wiped back to how it started and freed for someone else.

THE WELL

From tier 2, the plot's well produces one Ice Shmeckle Shmacker a day while rent is paid. Collect them from /plot. That's the only new source of melon seeds anywhere — trade them, plant them, sell them.

THE TENANT

KEEP IT ALIVE

Reaching tier 3 moves a Tenant into chunk 3. It can't leave, and only you and your members can hurt it.

If it dies you lose that week's reward. Revive it from /plot for 192 SBT (3 stacks).

THE COSY ROOM

  • 9 carpets, any colour
  • 32 oak logs
  • 1 campfire (or soul campfire)
  • 1 block of gold

All somewhere in chunk 3.

At the Sunday check, a plot with rent paid, a living tenant and a full cosy room earns a Wheel Token. Right-click it to spin the same wheel as a Wither kill.

FROZEN TOKENS

FROZEN SHMECKLE BLOCK TOKENS

What Ice Shmackers grow. Frozen tokens are worthless and delicate — pistons smash them, so they're built for auto-farms. Regular tokens can't be pushed or broken by pistons.

THAWING THEM OUT

A smashed Frozen token drops Shmackaroons. Craft 9 into a Cracked Shmeckle Block Token, then smelt it in a furnace into a real one.

Shmackers are grey by default. Right-click them with a dye to recolour them.
