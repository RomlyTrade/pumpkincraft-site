Silver Coins

  • Used for paying for claims & plots.
  • Used for player to player trade.
  • Used to summon bosses.
  • Used to enforce /drown.

THE COIN FURNACE

  • Right-click a blast furnace. Coins go left, any fuel in the middle.
  • Smelt up: 9 Bronze → 1 Silver · 9 stacks of Silver → 1 Gold · 1 stack of Gold → 1 Platinum.
  • Smelt down: 1 Silver → 9 Bronze · 1 Gold → 9 stacks of Silver · 1 Platinum → 1 stack of Gold.
  • Gold and Platinum Coins pay rent, shops, plots and Menachem at full value. Change comes back in silver.

HOW TO EARN SILVER COINS?

  • Complete missions from Menachem on DASSOM.
  • Open a shop selling in-demand items.
  • Selling blocks and items to Menachem on his visits.
  • Farm them yourself using your Brass Feed Pipes, grow your farm with /seed.

EVERY KIND OF COIN

Silver Coin
Main currency.
Gold Coin
576 silver (one inventory row) in one. Place it like a slab; two make a full block.
Platinum Coin
64 Gold Coins (36,864 silver) in one.
Old Crate
Delicate and worthless until broken. Can be broken by pistons, which enables auto farming.
Bronze Coins
Dropped by Old Crates.
Cracked Silver Coin
Needs to be smelted before turning into a usable Silver Coin.
