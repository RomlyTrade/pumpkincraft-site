Shmeckle Block Token - SBT

  • Used for paying for claims & plots.
  • Used for player to player trade.
  • Used to summon bosses.
  • Used to enforce /drown.

HOW TO EARN SHMECKLE BLOCK TOKENS?

  • Complete missions from Menachem on DASSOM.
  • Open a shop selling in-demand items.
  • Farm them yourself using your Shmeckle Shmackers, grow your farm with /seed.

EVERY KIND OF SBT

Shmeckle Block Token
Main currency.
Shuper Shmeckle
1,728 SBTs (a single chest) in one.
Frozen Shmeckle Block Token
Frozen and delicate form of SBT. Can be broken by pistons, which enables auto farming.
Shmackaroons
Fragments of the Frozen SBT.
Broken Shmeckle Block Token
Needs to be smelted before turning into a usable Shmeckle Block Token.
