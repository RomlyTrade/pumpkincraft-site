1YOUR FIRST DAY

  1. Join on Java at oceanworld2.com, or Bedrock at play.oceanworld2.com port 19132.
  2. Find a spot on Altis you want to live, then /claim it.
  3. Place your rent barrel and name your claim.
  4. Start earning silver (see making money) and keep your barrel topped up so your base stays protected.

2CLAIMING

CLAIMS

Our unique claims system works like so:

/claim will protect the cubic chunk you are stood in. That's 16×16×16.

To add your friends: /claim trust <player> <rank>

After your first cubic chunk, you must place your barrel, which is where you pay your rent into.

Expand your claim by /claiming the neighbouring cubic chunks. The price of every cube is added up at your barrel.

If you do not pay your rent by Sunday 18:00 GMT, your claim will be dissolved, and no longer protected from other players.

3COINS

Silver Coins are the money of OceanWorld2. You pay rent with them, trade with other players, summon bosses, /drown people and buy from Menachem.

Silver Coin — the everyday coin.
Gold Coin — 576 silver (one inventory row).
Platinum Coin — 64 gold (36,864 silver).

KEEP YOUR SILVER SAFE

Your claim barrel only holds up to 90 days of your rent. Anything over that has to be placed down as coins somewhere in your base.

Placed coins can be picked up by anyone, even inside your claim. Build them a secure room.

THE COIN FURNACE

Right-click a blast furnace to change coins up or down a tier: 9 stacks of silver make 1 gold, 1 stack of gold makes 1 platinum, and back again. Gold and platinum pay rent, shops, plots and Menachem at full value, with change in silver.

4MENACHEM & THE ECONOMY

Menachem is the server's banker. Silver doesn't come from nowhere: it goes round in a circle between the players and Menachem.

YOU PAY MENACHEM

  • Claim rent and Stratis plots
  • Boss summons and /drown
  • Anything you buy from him
◀ ▶
MENACHEM'S
FUNDS

MENACHEM PAYS YOU

  • Contracts on DASSOM
  • Buying your items and blocks
  • Achievements and votes

TRAVELLING MENACHEM

Every so often Menachem turns up next to a player for about ten minutes with a random set of trades, buying and selling. Grab him while he's there.

CAPTAIN MENACHEM

Always on his yacht, south of Altis and Stratis. His stall changes every hour and some trades have a daily limit.

5HOW TO MAKE MONEY

CONTRACTS

Menachem posts jobs on DASSOM: build this, clear that, fetch these. Finish one and he pays out in silver.

OPEN DASSOM ▸

FARMING

Grow, breed and mob-farm on your base or rent a plot on Stratis, the only island where auto-farms work. Then sell what you make to players or Menachem.

STRATIS PLOTS ▸

SELLING

Sell mob drops, dirt, cobble and more to Menachem when he visits or on his yacht. Or open your own shop: hold the item, look at a chest and type /shop create <price>.

6VOICE CHAT

Talk to players near you in-game. Java only: Bedrock can't use it, and you can still play without it.

  1. Install a mod loader for your Minecraft version: Fabric or NeoForge.
  2. Download Simple Voice Chat for the same version and loader, and put it in your mods folder.
  3. Launch with that profile and join oceanworld2.com.
  4. Press V to pick your microphone. Hold Caps Lock to talk, or switch to voice activation in the same menu.
  5. Press G to make or join a group and talk from anywhere.
Prices and rules can change.
